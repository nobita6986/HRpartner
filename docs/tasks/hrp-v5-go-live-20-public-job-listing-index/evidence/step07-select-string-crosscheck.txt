Evidence per-AC dung `grep` de giu MOT duong ma hoa UTF-8 va de co ma thoat tien trinh that.
Tep nay chay lai cac phep dem chinh bang dung cu hop dong goi ten, de chung minh HAI dung cu
cho CUNG con so. Cot MATCHES la $m.Count cua Select-String tren ban Get-Content -Encoding UTF8.

--- HAI CAVEAT phai noi ro, khong duoc de nguoi doc tu suy:
  (1) CA HAI dung cu deu dem SO DONG co khop, khong phai so LAN khop: `Select-String` nhan tung dong
      tu pipeline, `grep -c` cung dem dong. O tep nay hai cach cho cung ket qua vi khong dong nao
      chua hai phan tu -- nhung do la mot su that ve TEP NAY, khong phai mot tinh chat cua dung cu.
      Hang rao trong lane moi la thu dem theo SO LAN KHOP (regex tren toan van ban), va do la con so
      dung de ghim AC-18; xem lai bai hoc "dem chuoi con khac dem token" cua go-live-16.
  (2) `Select-String` la cmdlet nen KHONG co ma thoat tien trinh: `MATCHES= 0` o day la mot con so toi
      tu in ra, khong phai mot ma thoat do he dieu hanh tra ve. Do la ly do goc khien duong evidence
      chinh nam ben `grep`, cho o "phai bang 0" co mot bang chung khong do toi tu khai.
